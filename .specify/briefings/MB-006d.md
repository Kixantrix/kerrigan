  CLI `--sandbox` alone is not SDK-session proof. Preserve deny-default MXC

  inventory/policy/denial cases and hash-bound receipt.
- AC-MB-003/004: A bounded action broker accepts only explicit task paths,
  unique action identities and finite byte/action budgets. Model-generated
  code runs only through the effective no-fork MXC boundary, never the host.
  An independent owner supervises the real CLI and task handles, persists
  starts before execution and retains bounded byte-exact events/times.
  Level: integration; environment: local-attested-macos; test: allowlist,
  duplicate/action/output-limit and execution-path cases.
- AC-MB-004: Actual integrated timeout/cancellation/controller-loss handling
  stops owned CLI/task execution with monotonic cutoff and 15-second cleanup

  no-fork boundary and verified live-handle termination. Recovery never launches
  a model/tool, signals guessed PIDs or claims quiescence without evidence.
  Level: integration; environment: local-attested-macos; test: direct and
  attempted detached-task cutoff/recovery cases.
- AC-MB-006/007: Once the integrated cases pass, run at most one tiny synthetic
  canary each for `mai-code-2` and `claude-sonnet-5.5`, 180 active seconds plus
  15 seconds cleanup. Required model pins and observed runtime/event identity

  These are the previously authorized two total canaries, not a new allowance,
  scored historical trials or a ranking.
  Level: scenario; environment: local-attested-macos; test: two canary receipts.
- AC-MB-001/003: Existing contracts, ledger/report, independent grader and task
  packages remain read-only. Synthetic candidate stdout or process exit cannot
  establish a trusted verdict. Preserve earlier failures/partial proofs and
  excluded credential-bearing state.
  Level: integration; environment: local-attested-macos; test: contract checks.
