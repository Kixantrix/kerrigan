# S3 — Platform plans and prices (USD)

| Plan | Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Free | 0.00 per app per month | Built-in sign-in with pre-configured providers and custom roles included. |
| Standard | 9.00 per app per month | Adds custom sign-in providers, password protection, SLA, larger quotas. |

The plan is chosen per app. A feature that needs the Standard plan is not
available to an app on the Free plan. Sign-in usage itself is not metered on
either plan.
